# CEO Personal Operating System

A private, single-user productivity system for executives who want clarity without complexity.

---

## What This Is

This is your personal operating system — a quiet, structured space for reflection, goal-setting, and maintaining clarity across the chaos of leadership.

It is not software. It is not a dashboard. It is not productivity theater.

It is a simple collection of markdown files, thoughtful prompts, and proven frameworks that help you:

- **Reflect** on what actually happened (not what you hoped would happen)
- **Decide** what matters most (and let go of what doesn't)
- **Track** your energy, not just your output
- **Design** your future with intention, not just ambition
- **Remember** what you've learned about yourself

---

## How This Works

Everything is plain text. You can edit any file with any text editor, notes app, or even in your terminal. There are no accounts, no syncing issues, no app to learn.

**The system has four rhythms:**

| Rhythm | Time Required | Purpose |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| Daily | 5 minutes | Capture energy, wins, friction |
| Weekly | 20-30 minutes | Identify patterns, make one adjustment |
| Quarterly | 2-3 hours | Evaluate goals, detect drift, course-correct |
| Annual | 4-6 hours | Deep reflection, life design, set new direction |

---

## Quick Start (Under 15 Minutes)

### 1. Personalize Your North Star (5 minutes)

Open `north_star.md` and answer the three questions. Don't overthink it. Your first instinct is usually right.

### 2. Skim the Principles (3 minutes)

Open `principles.md` and read through the operating principles. Edit anything that doesn't resonate. Add anything that's missing.

### 3. Start Your First Daily Check-In (5 minutes)

Copy the template from `reviews/daily/template.md` to a new file with today's date (e.g., `reviews/daily/2025-01-06.md`). Answer the five prompts.

### 4. You're Done

That's it. You now have a running system. Everything else — weekly reviews, quarterly planning, interviews — will emerge naturally when you need it.

## How to Use Each Section

### Daily Check-Ins (`reviews/daily/`)

**When:** Every morning or evening, whichever works for you
**Time:** 5 minutes maximum
**Purpose:** Maintain awareness of your energy and focus

Each day, create a new file (e.g., `2025-01-06.md`) using the template. Answer five simple prompts:

1. Energy level (1-10)
2. One meaningful win
3. One friction point
4. One thing to let go of
5. One priority for tomorrow

Don't journal. Don't analyze. Just capture.

---

### Weekly Reviews (`reviews/weekly/`)

**When:** Friday afternoon or Sunday evening
**Time:** 20-30 minutes
**Purpose:** Identify what actually moved the needle

Create a new file each week (e.g., `2025-W01.md`). The template guides you through:

1. What actually moved things forward?
2. What was noise disguised as work?
3. Where did time leak?
4. One strategic insight from the week
5. One adjustment for next week

The goal is one meaningful insight and one concrete adjustment.

---

### Quarterly Reviews (`reviews/quarterly/`)

**When:** Last week of each quarter
**Time:** 2-3 hours
**Purpose:** Evaluate goal progress, detect misalignment, course-correct

Create a file (e.g., `2025-Q1.md`) and work through:

1. Goal progress assessment (honest, not optimistic)
2. Energy vs. output analysis
3. Misalignment detection
4. Strategic course corrections
5. Quarterly theme or focus

This is where you catch drift before it becomes a problem.

---

### Annual Reviews (`reviews/annual/`)

**When:** Last week of December or first week of January
**Time:** 4-6 hours (can be split across multiple sessions)
**Purpose:** Deep reflection, life design, and setting the year's direction

The annual review uses multiple frameworks:

1. **Gustin Annual Review** — Structured reflection on what happened
2. **Life Map Update** — Check-in across six life domains
3. **Ideal Lifestyle Costing** — Reality-check your vision against resources
4. **Vivid Vision Refresh** — Paint a detailed picture of your future
5. **Year Narrative** — Write the story of your past year and intent for the next

Take your time. This is the most important review of the year.

---

### Interviews (`interviews/`)

These are guided self-inquiry scripts. Use them when you need to think through something important or when preparing for a major review.

- **Past Year Reflection** — Before your annual review
- **Identity and Values** — When feeling lost or at a crossroads
- **Future Self Interview** — When planning major life changes

Read the questions slowly. Answer in writing. Don't rush.

---

### Frameworks (`frameworks/`)

These are thinking tools, not checklists. Each framework includes:

- Simple explanation of the concept
- When to use it
- How to apply it
- Credit to the original source

Use them as needed. You don't need to use all of them.

---

### Goals (`goals/`)

Three time horizons help you maintain perspective:

- **1 Year** — Concrete, measurable, committed
- **3 Years** — Directional, ambitious, flexible
- **10 Years** — Visionary, aspirational, loose

Update your 1-year goals quarterly. Review 3-year and 10-year goals annually.

---

### Memory (`memory.md`)

This file accumulates insights about yourself over time. After each quarterly and annual review, add key learnings:

- Patterns you've noticed
- Strengths that consistently show up
- Blind spots you keep hitting
- Themes across years
- Questions that remain unresolved

The system references this file to help future-you remember what past-you learned.

---

### Uploads (`uploads/`)

Store historical documents here:

- **Past Annual Reviews** — Previous years' reflections, journal excerpts, or planning documents
- **Notes** — Miscellaneous captures, articles that resonated, feedback you've received

These feed into pattern recognition. When doing your annual review, you (or an AI assistant) can analyze these documents to surface themes and patterns.

---

## Uploading and Analyzing Past Documents

If you have past annual reviews, performance feedback, journal entries, or planning documents:

1. Place them in `uploads/past_annual_reviews/` or `uploads/notes/`
2. During your annual review, read through them looking for:
   - Repeated goals that keep appearing (pattern: something matters but isn't happening)
   - Repeated failures (pattern: blind spot or structural issue)
   - Repeated strengths (pattern: lean into these more)
   - Themes across years (pattern: what you're really working on)

3. Summarize key insights in `memory.md`

If using an AI assistant, ask it to:
> "Analyze the documents in my uploads folder. Look for repeated goals, recurring failures, consistent strengths, and themes across years. Create an Executive Pattern Summary and add key insights to memory.md."

## Design Principles

This system was built with these principles:

1. **Clarity over complexity** — Fewer tools, more thinking
2. **Reflection over tracking** — Quality of insight, not quantity of data
3. **Energy over time** — Manage yourself, not just your calendar
4. **Plain text over platforms** — Portable, future-proof, no lock-in
5. **Private over shared** — This is for you, not your board
6. **Gentle over aggressive** — Accountability without shame
7. **Realistic over idealistic** — What you'll actually do, not what you should do

## Adapting the System

Every file contains placeholders you can personalize:

- `[YOUR COMPANY]` — Replace with your company name
- `[YOUR ROLE]` — Replace with your title or role description
- `[YOUR STAGE OF LIFE]` — Replace with relevant context (e.g., "building my first company," "transitioning out of operating role")
- `[YOUR CURRENT PRIORITIES]` — Replace with what matters most right now

Spend 15 minutes on your first session personalizing these. After that, the system adapts to you through use.

## What Success Looks Like

After a month of use, you should feel:

- **Less mental clutter** — The system holds what you'd otherwise try to remember
