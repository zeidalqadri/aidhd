## How to Use This Document

Ten years is long. Most predictions are wrong. That's not the point.

The 10-year vision is about direction, not destination. It's the mountain on the horizon you're walking toward, not GPS coordinates.

Use this document to:
- Clarify what kind of life you're building
- Make major decisions that take years to pay off
- Stay focused when short-term noise threatens to derail you
- Reconnect with your deeper purpose

Review annually. Don't expect accuracy. Expect clarity.

## A Note on 10-Year Thinking

Nobody knows what will happen in 10 years. The technology, economy, and world will be different in ways we can't predict. Your life will take turns you don't expect.

That's fine. The purpose of 10-year thinking isn't prediction. It's:

1. **Direction** — A point on the horizon to walk toward
2. **Patience** — Permission to play long-term games
3. **Perspective** — Today's problems may not matter
4. **Ambition** — Compound effects are massive over 10 years
5. **Anti-fragility** — Building for the long term, not just next quarter

Update this document as you learn. The vision will evolve. That's the point.
