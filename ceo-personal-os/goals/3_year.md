## A Note on 3-Year Planning

Three years is long enough for significant change and short enough to be somewhat predictable. But life has a way of disrupting plans.

Hold these goals loosely. Update them as you learn. The value isn't in perfect prediction — it's in having a direction that guides daily and yearly decisions.

When you're unsure what to do today, ask: "What moves me toward my 3-year vision?"
