# Past Year Reflection Interview

A guided conversation with yourself about the year that was.

---

## How to Use This

Set aside 60-90 minutes in a quiet space. Read each question slowly. Write your answers by hand if possible — it slows you down and opens different thinking.

This is not an exam. There are no right answers. The goal is insight.

Some questions will be easy. Some will be uncomfortable. Both are useful.

---

## Before You Begin

Take three deep breaths. Let go of whatever you were just doing.

This is time for you. Not your company. Not your team. Not your obligations.

Just you, looking honestly at your year.

---

## Part 1: The Highlights

Let's start with what went well.

### 1. Tell me about the last year — highlights first.

What were the genuinely good moments? Not accomplishments, but moments when you felt alive, proud, or deeply satisfied.

*Write freely:*




### 2. What was your biggest win?

The thing that, if someone asked "how was your year?" this might be the first thing you'd mention.




### 3. What are you proud of that no one else sees?

The private victories. The inner shifts. The things you handled that no one knows about.




### 4. Where did you exceed your own expectations?

Where did you surprise yourself?




### 5. What relationship deepened this year?

Who did you grow closer to?




---

## Part 2: The Hard Stuff

Now let's look at what didn't go as planned.

### 6. What drained you the most this year?

Not what was hardest — what depleted you? What took more than it gave?




### 7. What disappointed you?

About your results, your company, yourself, or others.




### 8. Where did you fall short of your own standards?

Be honest. No one sees this but you.




### 9. What did you avoid that you knew you should face?

The hard conversation you didn't have. The decision you delayed. The truth you didn't want to see.




### 10. What would you not repeat under any circumstances?

If you could remove one experience, situation, or pattern from ever happening again, what would it be?
