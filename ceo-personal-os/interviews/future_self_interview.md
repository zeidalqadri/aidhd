# Future Self Interview

A conversation with the person you're becoming.

---

## Overview

This is an imaginative exercise that accesses wisdom you already have but may not be hearing.

You'll conduct an interview with yourself 10 years from now — a version of you who has successfully navigated the current challenges and built the life you're working toward.

This isn't woo-woo. It's practical psychology. You have intuitions and wisdom that rational analysis doesn't access. This exercise opens that channel.

---

## When to Use This

- When facing a major decision
- When feeling stuck or directionless
- During annual planning
- When you need perspective you don't currently have
- When you're being too short-term in your thinking

**Time required:** 60-90 minutes

---

## Preparation

Find a quiet space. Close your eyes for a few minutes.

Imagine yourself 10 years from now. You've navigated the current challenges successfully. You've made good decisions. You're living a life you're proud of.

Picture this person clearly:
- Where are they?
- What do they look like?
- How do they carry themselves?
- What's their energy like?

This is the person you're about to interview.

---

## The Interview

### Opening

**You (present self):** Hello. Thank you for making time for this conversation. I'm you, but from 10 years ago. I'm at [YOUR CURRENT STAGE OF LIFE], running [YOUR COMPANY], and I'm trying to make good decisions. Can you help me?

**Future Self:** [Imagine their response. Write what they would say.]




---

### Part 1: Big Picture

**You:** Looking back at where I am right now, what do you wish I understood?




**You:** What was I worried about that turned out not to matter?




**You:** What was I not worried about that I should have been paying attention to?




**You:** What was the most important decision I made in the next few years?




**You:** What decision did I almost get wrong? What helped me see clearly?




---

### Part 2: Career and Work

**You:** Tell me about your work. What happened with [YOUR COMPANY]?




**You:** What did I need to learn as a leader?




**You:** What was I tolerating that I shouldn't have?




**You:** What turned out to be the highest-leverage use of my time?




**You:** What was I spending time on that didn't matter?




**You:** What advice do you have about how I'm running [YOUR COMPANY] right now?




---

### Part 3: Relationships

**You:** Tell me about the important people in your life. Who is still here?




**You:** What relationships did I underinvest in?




**You:** What relationships did I overinvest in?




**You:** Is there someone I should be paying more attention to right now?




**You:** What do you wish I understood about my closest relationships?




---

### Part 4: Health and Energy

**You:** How's your health? How's your body?




**You:** What did I do right regarding health in the coming years?




**You:** What did I get wrong? What do I wish I'd changed sooner?




**You:** What should I know about maintaining energy for the long game?




---

### Part 5: Personal Growth

**You:** How have I changed in 10 years?




**You:** What did I need to let go of?




**You:** What did I need to develop?




**You:** What was the most important inner work I did?




**You:** What books, teachers, or experiences shaped me most?




---

### Part 6: The Hard Questions

**You:** What am I pretending not to know right now?




**You:** What am I afraid of that I need to face?




**You:** Where am I playing too small?




**You:** Where am I being reckless or overconfident?




**You:** What's the conversation I need to have that I'm avoiding?




**You:** What would you do if you were me right now?




---

### Part 7: Values and Purpose

**You:** What matters most to you now?




**You:** Has what matters changed, or just deepened?




**You:** What's your biggest regret?




**You:** What are you most proud of?




**You:** What's your purpose now? Has it evolved?




---

### Part 8: Practical Wisdom

**You:** What's one thing I should start doing immediately?




**You:** What's one thing I should stop doing immediately?




**You:** What's one thing I should continue but give more attention to?




**You:** What's the trap I'm most likely to fall into?




**You:** What would you tell me about the next 12 months specifically?




---

### Closing

**You:** Is there anything else you want me to know?




**You:** Any final words?




**Future Self:** [Write their closing message to you]
