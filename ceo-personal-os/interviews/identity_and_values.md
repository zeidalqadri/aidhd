# Identity and Values Interview

A guided exploration of who you are and what you stand for.

---

## When to Use This

- When you feel lost or at a crossroads
- Before making a major life decision
- When your current path feels misaligned
- Every few years as a check-in on your core identity
- When you're asking yourself "Who am I, really?"

---

## How to Use This

Set aside 90 minutes minimum. Find a quiet space where you won't be interrupted.

These questions go deep. Answer honestly, not ideally. Who you are, not who you think you should be.

If a question doesn't resonate, skip it and come back. If a question triggers something, stay with it.

## After the Interview

This document is for you. It will evolve over time.

- Save this file with a date (e.g., `identity_and_values_2025.md`)
- Reference it before major decisions
- Revisit every 2-3 years or after major life changes

Identity isn't fixed. But having a clear articulation of who you are right now helps you make decisions that are aligned with that self.
