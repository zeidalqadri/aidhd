# North Star

Your North Star is your core direction — the fundamental orientation that guides major decisions and helps you recognize when you've drifted.

This is not a goal. Goals change. This is deeper.

---

## The Three Questions

Answer these simply and honestly. One sentence each.

### 1. What do you want your life to be about?

[YOUR ANSWER]

*Examples from other leaders:*
- *"Building things that outlast me."*
- *"Raising children who become good adults."*
- *"Creating freedom for myself and those I employ."*
- *"Solving problems most people think are impossible."*

---

### 2. What would you do if you couldn't fail and didn't need the money?

[YOUR ANSWER]

*This reveals what you actually care about, separate from fear and financial pressure.*

---

### 3. At the end of your life, what will matter most?

[YOUR ANSWER]

*This is the ultimate filter. Run major decisions through this.*

---

## Your North Star Statement

Synthesize the above into a single sentence or phrase that captures your direction:

> [YOUR NORTH STAR STATEMENT]

*Examples:*
- *"Build a company worth building, raise a family worth raising, stay healthy enough to enjoy both."*
- *"Create leverage that generates freedom, then use that freedom to create things that matter."*
- *"Master my craft, mentor the next generation, leave my corner of the world better than I found it."*

---

## Using Your North Star

Your North Star is useful in specific moments:

1. **Major career decisions** — Does this move me toward or away from my North Star?

2. **Saying no** — "This is great, but it doesn't align with my North Star" is a complete answer.

3. **Annual planning** — Does next year's plan move me closer to what my life is about?

4. **Detecting drift** — If you haven't thought about your North Star in months, you may be drifting.

5. **Recovery from setbacks** — Your North Star remains true even when specific goals fail.

---

## North Star vs. Goals

| North Star | Goals |
|------------|-------|
| Direction | Destination |
| Permanent (mostly) | Temporary |
| Why | What |
| Orientation | Achievement |
| Who you want to be | What you want to accomplish |

Both matter. Goals without a North Star lead to hollow achievement. A North Star without goals leads to beautiful inaction.

---

## When to Revisit

Revisit your North Star annually during your Annual Review.

It shouldn't change often. If it does, you're still discovering it — which is fine.

A genuine North Star shift might happen:
- After a major life event (birth, death, illness, divorce)
- After a significant success or failure that changes your perspective
- In a new decade of life
- After deep work in therapy, coaching, or self-inquiry

Mostly, your North Star remains constant while your strategies and tactics evolve.
