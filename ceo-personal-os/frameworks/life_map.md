# Life Map Framework

*Adapted from Alex Lieberman's Life Map approach. The core insight: your life is not just your career. Designing a great life means consciously allocating attention across all domains that matter.*

---

## Overview

As a CEO or founder, it's easy to let work consume everything. Career success comes at the expense of health, relationships suffer, and you wake up ten years later having won a game you're not sure you wanted to play.

The Life Map prevents this by making all life domains visible and intentional. You can't optimize what you don't measure. You can't balance what you don't see.

This is not about perfect balance — that's a myth. It's about conscious allocation.

---

## The Six Domains

### 1. Career / Work
What you do professionally. Your company, role, impact, income, growth, and mastery.

### 2. Relationships
Your partner, family, close friends, and community. The people who matter.

### 3. Health
Physical and mental. Your body, energy, longevity, and mind.

### 4. Meaning / Purpose
Why you do what you do. Contribution, legacy, spirituality, philosophy.

### 5. Finances
Money as a tool. Security, freedom, options, and generosity.

### 6. Fun / Joy
Play, adventure, hobbies, experiences. The parts of life that make it enjoyable.

---

## When to Use This

- During annual planning (comprehensive review)
- Quarterly (quick check-in)
- When something feels off but you can't name it
- When you've been overly focused on one domain

**Time required:** 1-2 hours for initial mapping, 30 minutes for updates

## Using Your Life Map

### Quarterly Check-Ins

Every quarter, revisit your scores. Are you drifting? Update and adjust.

### Decision Filter

Before major commitments, run them through the Life Map:
- Which domain does this serve?
- Which domains might it hurt?
- Is that tradeoff worth it?

### Compensation Watch

Notice when you're using one domain to compensate for another:
- Working more to avoid relationship problems
- Spending money to substitute for meaning
- Fun as an escape from career dissatisfaction

### Permission Slip

The Life Map gives you permission to invest in "non-productive" domains. Fun is not frivolous. Relationships are not soft. Health is not selfish.

## Common Patterns

**The All-In Career Pattern**
High career, low everything else. Common in founders and executives. Feels productive but leads to burnout and regret.

**The Relationship Sacrifice Pattern**
Career and finances look great. Relationships are suffering. Often invisible until a crisis.

**The Health Debt Pattern**
Borrowing from health to fund other domains. Works until it doesn't. Compounds badly.

**The Meaning Void**
Everything looks fine on paper but something feels empty. Usually a meaning/purpose gap.

**The Deferred Fun Pattern**
"I'll have fun when I'm successful." Success never feels like enough. Fun keeps getting deferred.

---

## A Note on Balance

Perfect balance is a myth. Some seasons require intense focus on one domain. That's fine — as long as it's conscious.

The goal isn't perfect balance. The goal is:
1. Awareness of all domains
2. Intentional allocation
3. No domain completely abandoned
4. Regular course correction

You're playing a long game. A year of intense career focus is fine if you know you're doing it and plan to rebalance.
